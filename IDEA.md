# Idea brief · Form Fatigue Fix

**Hackathon:** IdeaLab Hacks  
**URL:** https://idealab-hacks.devpost.com/  
**Deadline:** Monday, November 30, 2026, 11:45 PM America/Toronto  
**Solo entry by:** John Khan, Toronto, Ontario

## The problem
People delay official paperwork because a short form still hides three kinds of boxes: ones you must complete, ones you can skip, and ones you should not touch. The cost is wasted time and incomplete filings, not a lack of the PDF itself.

## The idea
Ship a small static site that already knows the field list of two real public forms. The user picks a form, sees required versus optional versus office use, and gets a one-page fill order. An optional photo of their own copy can sit beside the list for comfort. The site never claims to read the photo.

## Why this fits IdeaLab
The brief asks for a focused prototype that makes an everyday task easier. A field-accurate checklist is a small experiment with a clear before and after: a messy PDF versus a numbered order.

## Scope for this build
- Bundled samples only: Toronto FOI Access Request (07-0088 2023-01) and LTB Form N9 (v. 01/04/2022)
- Every field cited from the official PDF or guide
- Copyable fill order
- No accounts, no paid APIs, no invented data

## Out of scope
Submitting a request, giving legal advice, or guessing fields from a screenshot.

## User
A Toronto resident filling a city FOI request or an Ontario tenant preparing Form N9.

## Success
A judge can open the page, switch forms, see the labels change, and copy the order in one minute.
